    Start with your own context

    Keep your accounts, budgets and goals in one place. Net worth, forecasts and currencies are calculated on your computer, without AI.

    Private local inference

    Choose a local model

    Connect LM Studio, Ollama or another compatible local model server for Guru, advisor analyses and PDF statement import. Model processing stays on your machine.

    Only if you choose

    Choose an online service

    A compatible online AI endpoint processes the prompts and context you send to it outside your computer. Decision Maker uses a separately configured service. Review provider privacy terms; online AI is not local-only processing.

    Online research needs external sources. Loonie Cloud sync and Google Drive backup are optional and send data off your computer when enabled.
