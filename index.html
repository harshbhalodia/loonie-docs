AI that understands the bigger picture.

Loonie doesn't just look at one number. It connects the signals across your life to help you understand trade-offs, opportunities and next steps.

Deterministic math comes first — agents only explain numbers your data has already computed, never invent them. Bring your own local model, or run Loonie fully offline with AI disabled.

“Can I afford this trip without delaying my home goal?”
“Why did my spending increase this month?”
“What happens if I increase my savings by $300/month?”
“Which habits are helping me reach my goals?”

Your life belongs to you.

Loonie is designed around privacy, local-first data and user-controlled AI.

  • Local by default. A self-hosted FastAPI backend + SQLite database on your own machine.
  • Private by design. Nothing leaves your disk unless you explicitly point AI at a remote endpoint.
  • AI when you choose. Every calculation is deterministic and works with AI fully disabled.
  • No account. No subscription. No telemetry.

Get started in minutes

Windows 10/11. Python 3.11+ is installed automatically via winget if it's missing.

Open PowerShell and run:

irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/harshbhalodia/loonie/main/install.ps1 | iex
  • Installs Python automatically if it's missing (via winget)
  • Sets up the Loonie backend under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Loonie
  • Creates your local config with a unique, randomly generated security key
  • Downloads and launches the Loonie desktop installer

Free, open source, no account required. Prefer to install manually or read the source first? See the README on GitHub.
