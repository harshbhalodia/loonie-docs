    Always local

    Your calculations stay home

    Net worth, budgets, goals, forecasts and currencies are worked out on your computer. No AI needed.

    Private local inference

    AI on your computer

    Connect LM Studio, Ollama or another local model server to ask Guru, run advisor analyses or import PDF statements. Local inference keeps model processing on your machine.

    Only if you choose

    Online AI & services

    An online AI endpoint processes the prompts and context sent to it outside your computer. Decision Maker uses a separately configured service. Online research also needs external sources. Check each provider’s privacy terms; do not assume local-only privacy when connecting an online service.

    Loonie Cloud sync and Google Drive backup are also optional. Enabling either sends data off your computer.
