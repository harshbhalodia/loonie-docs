New · Blueprint Marketplace

Stress-test your finances like a bank would.

Install blueprints — advisory playbooks from Loonie and trusted publishers — and run them against your own profile: a rate shock, a job loss, a market correction, an inflation spike.

  • You choose what each blueprint sees. A consent screen lists every data category it asks for. Nothing unchecked is ever shared.
  • Every run is kept. Compare results over time and across blueprint versions, and rate what was helpful.
  • Publishers never receive your data. Blueprints run on your computer — only the advice logic is downloaded.
  • Free and premium. Start with Loonie's free core stress test; premium blueprints add deeper scenarios.
  • Build your own. Blueprint Studio lets you draft and test-run a blueprint with no code.

Your life belongs to you.

Privacy isn't a setting in Loonie. It's the architecture.

  • Local by default. Your data lives in a private database on your own computer.
  • Consent for every agent. Marketplace blueprints only see the categories you explicitly grant — enforced by the app, not just the screen.
  • AI when you choose. Every calculation works with AI turned off. Turn it on with a model that runs on your machine.
  • Sync only if you want it. Loonie Cloud is optional — nothing leaves your computer until you link an account. No telemetry.
