    When you need it

    AI on your computer

    Ask Guru privately to explain, stress-test or read a statement, using a model that runs on your own machine.

    Only if you choose

    Public AI

    Used for one thing: the online decision engine behind “Should I do A or B?”. It is off until you turn it on in Settings. Then only a summary of your figures, never your transactions, is sent.
